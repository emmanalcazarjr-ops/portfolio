## 📱 Section 4: Live Demonstration Playbook (Step-by-Step)

When pitching in person, always pull out your phone and run this sequence:

1. **Step 1:** Open your **Customer Bot** on your phone.
2. **Step 2:** Tap `/start` ➔ Show how fast it asks for:
   * Tower (e.g., Tower 1)
   * Unit (e.g., Unit 1204)
   * Type (Delivery vs Pickup)
   * Time (e.g., Today 2:00 PM)
3. **Step 3:** Tap **Confirm Order**.
4. **Step 4:** Immediately switch to (or have them look at) the **Operator Bot**.
5. **Step 5:** Show the incoming alert with the sound and the **"✅ Mark Done"** button.
6. **Step 6:** Tap **Mark Done** and show how the Customer Bot instantly receives: *"Your order is completed / out for delivery!"*

## 🛡️ Section 5: Handling Common Client Objections

### Objection 1: *"Why not just use Facebook Messenger or Viber?"*
> **Answer:** *"Messenger chats get mixed in with personal messages, promos, and spam. Your staff has to manually ask 'What unit? How many gallons? What time?' on every single order. This bot does that questioning automatically in 5 seconds and organizes all pending orders into a clean checklist."*

### Objection 2: *"My staff is not tech-savvy."*
> **Answer:** *"If your staff knows how to read a text message, they already know how to use this. They only have to look at the notification and tap one button: 'Mark Done'. Everything else is automatic."*

### Objection 3: *"What if some of my customers don't have Telegram?"*
> **Answer:** *"Telegram is free, takes 15 seconds to install, and uses almost zero phone storage. For customers who prefer to order through Telegram, you give them a VIP instant-ordering experience. For walk-ins, you can still take orders normally or register them with one tap."*
