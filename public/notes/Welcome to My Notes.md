# Welcome to My Notes

These notes live in my Obsidian vault (my whole `AI` workspace). Every time I save, the vault auto-syncs to GitHub, and my portfolio's **Notes** section updates automatically.

## How it works

1. I write here in Obsidian (markdown).
2. The **Obsidian Git** plugin auto-commits and pushes on a timer.
3. A GitHub Action copies `notes/` into the portfolio repo.
4. Vercel redeploys the site — done.

- Project notes and case studies
- Automation workflows I'm building
- Learnings from tools like n8n, Supabase, and AI APIs
